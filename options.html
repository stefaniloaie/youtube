Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) & Server Configuration
Google AI Studio API Key chrome.storage.sync
Supply your personal, free Google AI Studio key. Stored in chrome.storage.sync so it automatically syncs across all your Chrome browsers, and forwarded to the backend to run Gemini 2.5 Flash on your free quota.

Shared Default: If left blank, your requests will safely fall back to the backend's shared GEMINI_API_KEY running gemini-2.5-flash (leveraging its massive 1M context window and free tier inclusion).
How to get your free key:
  1. Visit Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com).
  2. Click Create API Key (100% free tier, 15 RPM with Gemini 2.5 Flash).
  3. Paste it above and click Save Settings.
